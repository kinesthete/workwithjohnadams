Four steps, from applying to your first session.

  1. You apply.

    About ten minutes, any time through Wednesday, October 7.

  2. I reach out.

    I’ll read your application and reach out to set up a short call.

  3. We talk.

    A short call, so we can both feel whether this is the right fit.

  4. You begin.

    The cohort starts in mid-October. I’ll confirm the venue and the start week before you enroll.

Here’s what the ten weeks hold.

  • Ten individual Structural Integration sessions with me, about two hours each, once a week.
  • Ten Wednesday-evening group sessions, two and a half hours each.
  • Journaling prompts between sessions, and two guided somatic meditations each week.
  • 5–6 hours of attention a week, for ten weeks.
  • Tuition of $4,500, with a limited number of supported-rate places.
