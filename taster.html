Your body is sending signals, to you and to everyone around you.

The way you inhabit your body is structuring your life. It’s the underlying feeling of your identity: what it’s like to be you.

Through it, you hold the patterns of your life in place.

Together we’ll take a closer look at these dynamics, between your bodily experience and your relational contexts.

And we’ll explore what becomes possible when we bring exquisite curiosity to layers of reality so close to us that we rarely appreciate them.

If you’re a therapist, feel it in your own body first, then decide if it’s for your clients.

If you’re a therapist, the fall cohort may suit a client of yours.

You likely have clients who have developed significant self-understanding, and who would benefit from extending that development into present-moment practice, in the embodied and relational realms.

Come explore this in your own body, and see if it’s a good fit, for you or for them.
